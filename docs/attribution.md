## Bird reference

Laitche, *Tree Sparrow August 2007 Osaka Japan.jpg*, 31 August 2007. The [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tree_Sparrow_August_2007_Osaka_Japan.jpg) states that the author released the photograph into the public domain. This was verified during the source research, not asserted from a filename alone.

The bird examples are simplified studies informed by that photograph. Their perched pose and rendering are adapted. The photograph is not reproduced in this repository.

## Methodology sources

[Ctrl+Paint](https://www.ctrlpaint.com/library) and [John Muir Laws' written drawing-birds tutorial](https://johnmuirlaws.com/drawing-birds/) informed the construction vocabulary. Public author/publisher pages for Molly Bang, Scott McCloud, Stephen Silver, Josef Albers, and Bruce Block informed the reading priorities.

The skill's workflow is the project's synthesis. Full books were not read or bundled. Lesson-page descriptions were read; most embedded instructional videos were not reviewed. The older Laws block-in sequence must not be presented as his latest revised method.

See the [source ledger](../skills/cionejr/references/sources-and-limits.md) for scope and limitations.

## Dependencies and fonts

Pillow, NumPy, and PyYAML are external dependencies with their own licenses. Installation uses the packages named in requirements.txt; their source is not vendored.

The bird label renderer attempts to load DejaVu Serif through Pillow's normal font lookup and falls back to Pillow's built-in font. No proprietary font is required or distributed. The label type is not part of a brand-fidelity guarantee.
