The cionejr workflow is a project synthesis. Do not attribute it to one author or imply endorsement by any source listed here.

## Circle-based logo reconstruction

[10 Famous Logos Created Using Only Circles, Yes I'm a Designer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=afKvcG1ezh4), uploaded 28 February 2022, duration 21:26. Native captions and selected frames were inspected. The Twitter reconstruction begins around 19:30.

The video supports studying shape operations on existing marks. It does not establish the original designers' intentions or ideation process, and must not be described as released before 2020. Video/caption files are not distributed here.

## Reference use

Record the creator, original URL, license/permission, and modifications for reference images. Observation does not automatically permit reproducing a photograph in public output. A drawing substantially informed by a photograph should disclose that relationship.

Detail references may be combined only when their relevant anatomy, material, and orientation are compatible. A downloaded image does not certify its species label or reproduction rights.

## Do not overstate evidence

Full books listed above have not been read in this project. Do not invent chapter content, page numbers, exercises, or claims of catalog availability. If a source cannot be accessed, say what remains unknown rather than guessing.

The sample renderers are not proof that instructions consistently improve model output. File checks, agent visual critique, and real viewer responses are distinct kinds of evidence. None demonstrates drawing mastery across every style or subject.
